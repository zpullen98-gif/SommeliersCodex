## Fixes by kind
- **Geography and facts (5):** the Bopparder Hamm is the steep wall on the outer side of the Rhine's loop, facing Filsen at its apex, not inside it (the place note and the card). Kaub faces Pfalzgrafenstein, which stands in the river, rather than lying below it. Virginia reaches from the Chesapeake Bay west over the Blue Ridge into the Appalachians, not only as far as the Blue Ridge.
- **Volatile wording (1):** the undated list of Virginia's AVAs now reads include rather than are; all nine are still named.
- **Depth tags (1):** an untagged Virginia card no longer teaches that the Shenandoah Valley AVA is shared with West Virginia, the fact the tagged question and the Premier Cru exam line ask.
- **Lines brought into line (2):** the Mittelrhein's Premier Cru exam line names Lorch, which its tagged question asks. A Virginia card names the Valley beside the mountains for winter cold, as the climate section and a question's explanation do.
- **Respellings (1):** Loudoun is LOUD-un.
