# c3 refuter review: Emilia-Romagna, Marche and Lazio

I checked all three articles in `italy.more.json` against standard knowledge, the Codex's own Village, Premier Cru and Grand Cru banks, the Court's lombardy-emilia-romagna, central-southern-italy and world-sparkling-wines rows, and seven searches (four for Emilia-Romagna, one for the Marche, two for Lazio). All three are sound and readable at the Village level, in the house style of the c1 and c2 neighbours. I made fourteen fixes in place and rewrote nothing that was right.

## Fixes by kind
- **Facts and precision (6):** The broader Pignoletto DOC has been the Emilia-Romagna DOC since the 2024 harvest. The Colli Bolognesi note now says so and gives the DOCG's first name, Colli Bolognesi Classico Pignoletto, changed in 2014. Lambrusco Mantovano is no longer said to come from across the Po, since much of its zone lies south of the river. La Stoppa's Ageno is a white led by Malvasia di Candia, not a varietal one. The Marche summary had Montepulciano alone on the Conero, where up to 15 per cent Sangiovese is allowed. Only the Jesi Riserva's name is reordered, not Matelica's. Lazio's four volcanic districts are now its four main ones, since the Piglio's volcanic soil comes from a fifth, smaller field.
- **Depth tags (3):** An untagged Marche exam line set Offida and Serrapetrona, which are tagged places, as Village study; it now says they are asked from Premier Cru. Lacrima di Morro d'Alba's card and question go from master to advanced, matching their place. Lazio's Malvasia Puntinata card is tagged advanced, because only the Grand Cru bank asks that name.
- **House rules (5):** The DOCG counts of all three regions are dated 2026, in the law prose and in Lazio's card and exam line. Paltrinieri loses a superlative. Cavicchioli loses family house, a claim about the current owner. Reggiano gets a respelling.

## Confirmed by search
- Gutturnio became a DOC of its own on 21 July 2010: Barbera 55 to 70 per cent, Croatina 30 to 45.
- Grechetto Gentile, Grechetto di Todi, Rebola and Alionzina are names of one vine, so the accept list stands.
- Rosso Piceno is Montepulciano 35 to 85 per cent with Sangiovese 15 to 50.
- Lazio has three DOCGs with no addition in 2025. The Roma DOC's Bianco and its Malvasia Puntinata wine stand as written.

## The Court's rows
Every row the Court writes for Emilia-Romagna is covered: the location, the climate, the Apennines, the styles and methods, the grape, the classification, the three Lambrusco subzones, the sweetness words, service, pairing and the by the glass row. For the Marche, the two Verdicchio DOCs, the climate, the Apennines and the Adriatic, the two grapes and the label terms are covered. For Lazio, the Tyrrhenian, the climate, the Apennines, the classification and the label terms are covered.
