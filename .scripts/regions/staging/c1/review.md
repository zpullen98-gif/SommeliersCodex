# c1 refuter review: Jura, and Savoie and Bugey

Two articles were checked in `france.more.json` against standard knowledge, the Codex's own Village, Premier Cru and Grand Cru banks and primers, and seven searches (three for the Jura, four for Savoie). Both are sound, readable at the Village level and close to the house style of the c2 neighbour. Ten fixes were made in place. Nothing that was right was rewritten.

## Fixes by kind
- **Depth tags (3):** Trousseau at Montigny-lès-Arsures and as Bastardo is asked in the Codex's Village bank, so its card and question lose their tag. The four Roussette de Savoie crus (three places, a card and a question) are asked in the Premier Cru bank, so their tag goes from master to advanced.
- **Facts (1):** Chignin makes reds as well as Jacquère whites, so the trap, the card and the place note now say chiefly a Jacquère white, with some red.
- **Question form (3):** in three questions the key was the longest option. A distractor of the same kind replaced the shorter one: Jura Q3 (Crémant du Jura), Savoie Q2 (Mondeuse Blanche) and Savoie Q5 (Fourteen).
- **Precision (1):** L'Étoile's star-fossil name is now given as the usual account.
- **Respelling (1):** Pupillin is now respelt pew-pee-YAN.
- **Currency (1):** the source for the Vin de Savoie text now carries its order of 14 June 2023 (BO-AGRI, 6 July 2023).

## Confirmed by search
