- **Question craft (4):** a Canary key was the longest option (The Cape Doctor replaces The levante); a Canary explanation no longer claims that no rule bans rootstocks; the Arabako stem asks for a province, so Navarra is a distractor of the same kind; chalk and albariza were one soil twice in a Valdeorras question, and Limestone replaces Chalk.

- **Cards (1):** the Canary card on the black grapes now asks for them, which its back answers.
- **Wording (1):** the Cantabrian Sea is the Bay of Biscay's Spanish shore, and the Txakoli water section names the Atlantic Ocean the Court gives.
