## 1. Which châteaux are the five Premiers Crus of Sauternes' 1855 list after Yquem's Supérieur? Name three.

**Verdict reasoning:** I tried to find a defensible reading and could not. Line 22 of data-master.js: the stem says 'five Premiers Crus' while the question's own exp field says 'Eleven premiers in all' — the question contradicts itself on its face. Eleven is the correct modern count (the original 1855 list had nine; Peyraguey split into Lafaurie-Peyraguey and Clos Haut-Peyraguey, and Rabaud split into Rabaud-Promis and Sigalas-Rabaud). Separately, the auditor is right that Rieussec is absent from both the accept array and the ans string: the long accept token lists exactly ten estates (Climens, La Tour Blanche, Lafaurie-Peyraguey, Clos Haut-Peyraguey, Rayne-Vigneau, Suduiraut, Coutet, Guiraud, Rabaud-Promis, Sigalas-Rabaud). I traced this through matchSA in core.js to confirm the grading consequence is real rather than theoretical: an answer of 'Rieussec, Climens, Coutet' normalizes to a 3-word string that fails exact match, fails whole-phrase containment against either accept entry, fails the 60%-word-coverage core test against the 19-word accept token, and fails the substring fallback (it is not contiguous inside either accept). So a candidate naming one of the eleven most famous premiers is graded incorrect. Two distinct defects, both confirmed.

**Fix:** Stem: "Which châteaux are the eleven Premiers Crus of Sauternes-Barsac's 1855 list beneath Yquem's Premier Cru Supérieur? Name three." Add Rieussec to the accept array (both as its own token and inside the long roster string) and to the ans: "e.g., Climens, Suduiraut, Coutet (also La Tour Blanche, Lafaurie-Peyraguey, Clos Haut-Peyraguey, Rayne-Vigneau, Rieussec, Guiraud, Rabaud-Promis, Sigalas-Rabaud)."

## 2. White Pessac-Léognan's classified pair of neighbors on the Haut-Brion plateau are Haut-Brion Blanc and:

**Verdict reasoning:** Upheld, though narrower than the auditor implies. The keyed answer is correct — La Mission Haut-Brion Blanc (formerly Laville Haut-Brion) is indeed the neighbor, and it is a classified white of Graves. The defect is confined to the stem's word 'classified' applied to the pair. The Graves classification (1953, whites appended 1959) lists nine classified whites: Bouscaut, Carbonnieux, Domaine de Chevalier, Couhins, Couhins-Lurton, Latour-Martillac, Laville Haut-Brion, Malartic-Lagravière, Olivier. Château Haut-Brion is classified for red only (in both 1855 and the Graves list); its Blanc carries no classification. That omission is itself a stock exam gotcha, so the stem inverts the very fact a candidate is likely to be asked. I considered defending this as loose descriptive prose rather than an assertion, but in a stem written to be memorized it functions as an assertion. Note the fix is a one-word stem edit — the accept array and ans need no change.

**Fix:** Stem: "Haut-Brion Blanc's neighbor and rival on the Pessac plateau — the classified white of the pair — is:" (or simply drop 'classified': "The white counterpart neighboring Haut-Brion Blanc on the Haut-Brion plateau is:"). Optionally extend exp to make the exam point explicit: "Sémillon-led, among the world's priciest dry whites; note Haut-Brion Blanc itself is unclassified — only Laville/La Mission Blanc appears among the nine crus classés de Graves for white."

## 8. Vendanges Tardives minimum must weights are set per variety. Which two varieties carry the higher bar?

**Verdict reasoning:** Confirmed at js/data-advanced.js:93. ## 19. Cava's premium tier since 2017, requiring 30 months and estate discipline, is:

**Verdict reasoning:** Confirmed and unambiguous. Cava de Paraje Calificado, created in 2017, requires a minimum of 36 months on the lees. 30 months is the Cava Gran Reserva minimum. As written the app makes CPC numerically identical to Gran Reserva, which erases the single most testable number about the tier. The error appears in three places, not one: data-advanced.js:207 (stem "requiring 30 months"), data-primers-advanced.js:175 ("9/18/30-month tiers plus Cava de Paraje Calificado (30 months)"), and data-primers-advanced.js:364, a sparkling-minimums cheat sheet that reads "Cava 9 / Reserva 18 / Gran Reserva 30 / Paraje Calificado 30" — the last one is the most damaging because it is a side-by-side comparison table where the collision is visible and still wrong.
