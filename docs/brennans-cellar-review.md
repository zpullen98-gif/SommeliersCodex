# Brennan's cellar training review

Reviewed 15 October 2026. The Cellar Compass turns the saved wine collection
into a practical path from recognition to recommendation.

## What was reviewed

The structural review covers all 271 developed wine records in the canonical
Brennan's pack: 34 menu or featured wines and 237 floor bottles. Each has
producer and component connections. The linked printed cellar reference is
much larger, with thousands of rows spanning vintages and formats. Neither
the printed reference nor these cards is a live inventory.

The factual review is targeted, not a claim that every bottle was checked
against its producer. Priority was given to featured wines, service rules,
contradictory data and the wines used in the new lessons. Producer technical
sheets, regional bodies and WSET material support the teaching. The exact
links are in each lesson's Sources and further reading and in
`js/data-cellar-compass.js`.

## Improvements

- A searchable directory reaches every developed wine, with collection,
  section and study-path filters. It retains the existing wine cards,
  producer histories, component lessons, maps and progress.
- A balanced daily selection brings five menu wines and five floor bottles
  into practice. Reading and revealing notes never creates a grade.
- Twelve guided studies build listening, sparkling service, white and red
  wine comparisons, sweetness, bottle care and list literacy.
- Sixty recall prompts ask the learner to explain the reasoning aloud.
- Eight fall-menu workshops cover every uploaded dish, comparing two
  approaches with a guest question, trade-off and lineup checks.

The visual treatment extends the existing forest-green, gold and parchment
design. Existing reviewed service and culinary illustrations are reused.
They teach general practice; they are not evidence of Brennan's current SOP.

## Corrections and evidence boundaries

The canonical World Table pipeline carries the bottle corrections across
the suite. Important examples are Rubicon 2010's four-grape blend; the
difference between finished-wine sugar and the sugar added at dosage;
overlap between Brut and Extra Brut categories; the distinction between
current producer blend descriptions and an unverified older bottle or
private-label cuvee; and Domaine Fichet's founding and family dates.

For Rubicon 2010 the estate archive lists 86 per cent Cabernet Sauvignon,
6 per cent Cabernet Franc, 5 per cent Petit Verdot and 3 per cent Merlot.
Moulin d'Issan is Bordeaux Superieur, not the estate's Margaux wine. Its
2021 estate sheet identifies 90 per cent Merlot and 10 per cent Cabernet
Sauvignon. Neither a dominant grape nor a regional name guarantees a
particular tasting outcome in today's bottle.

The linked restaurant pages and printed list disagree on some details.
Examples include the house Piper glass price, Rare's vintage and Moulin's
vintage. The review preserves the saved record's identity and teaches the
learner to check the actual label, current service list and lineup. An old
price or vintage is not silently promoted to current availability.

Pairing workshops are reasoned proposals based on the uploaded food sheets
and saved wine profiles. They are not an approved tasting-menu pairing or
a claim that the exact bottle and plate were tasted together. Sauce, spice,
sweetness, preparation, guest preference and bottle condition can change
the recommendation. Confirm allergies with the kitchen independently of
the wine exercise.

## A useful study routine

1. Open Today's Study and choose Your cellar study.
2. Study the ten selected wine cards; reveal the answer before self-grading.
3. Read one guided study and say the five responses aloud before opening
   their notes. Follow a wine into its producer, region or components.
4. Choose one fall pairing workshop. Explain both approaches without
   promising a flavour, price or serving format you have not confirmed.
5. Bring one unresolved bottle or menu question to lineup. Practise a short
   guest description that starts with their preference.

## Validation contract

The data gate verifies path and prompt identities, all wine/illustration/
lesson links, all eight canonical fall-dish IDs, source references and key
factual distinctions. The full-script browser gate checks real grading,
daily progression, reload, import/export/reset/rename, routes and Back,
search and mobile day/night layouts. Release also runs the public suite's
required gates and checks the published offline edition.
