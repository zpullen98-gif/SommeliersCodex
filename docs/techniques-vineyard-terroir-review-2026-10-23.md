## Evidence and limits by lesson

### Phylloxera and rootstocks

Scion and rootstock identity, strain-dependent response and hygiene. Grafting is not breeding, pest elimination or a quality grade. The lesson does not issue movement permits.

- [Phylloxera, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/viticulture/pests-and-diseases/phylloxera/)
- [Rootstocks and the rootstock selector, Vinehealth Australia](https://vinehealth.com.au/tools/rootstocks/)
- [Cultura da Vinha, Instituto dos Vinhos do Douro e do Porto](https://ivdp.pt/pt/vinha/cultura-da-vinha/)

### Organic and biodynamic

Distinguishes EU and United States organic claims, added sulphites and finished wine certification. Demeter's prescribed methods are described as certification requirements; claimed cosmic mechanisms are not presented as demonstrated sensory causes. No health, vegan or sulphite-free promise.

- [Organic production and products, European Commission](https://agriculture.ec.europa.eu/farming/organic-farming/organic-production-and-products_en)
- [International Demeter Biodynamic Standard, 2026](https://demeter.net/certification/standard/)
- [Labelling organic wine, USDA and TTB](https://www.ttb.gov/system/files/images/pdfs/wine-labeling-guide.pdf)
- [Demeter International: 2026 production, processing and labelling standard](https://demeter.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2026_Int_Dem_bio_Standard_eng.pdf)

### Frost

Separates radiation and advective events, the need for an inversion, and heat released as water freezes. No equipment operating prescription or universal temperature threshold. Crop loss and scarcity are not proof of wine quality.

- [Frost protection, British Columbia Wine Grape Council](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/vineyard-maintenance/frost-protection/)
- [Vineyard site selection, North Carolina State Extension](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/north-carolina-winegrape-growers-guide/chapter-4-vineyard-site-selection)
- [Understanding and preventing spring frost and freeze damage to grapes, Penn State Extension](https://extension.psu.edu/understanding-and-preventing-spring-frost-and-freeze-damage-to-grapes)

### Heat, drought and irrigation

Distinguishes heat, water shortage and monitored deficit strategies. Pressure-chamber measurements complement soil observations. Berry dehydration is not equated with flavour maturity. Permissions remain site-specific.

- [Managing vines through heatwaves, Wine Australia](https://www.wineaustralia.com/growing-making/environment-and-climate/managing-vines-through-heatwaves)
- [Monitoring vine water status, New South Wales Department of Primary Industries](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/1158125/Monitoring-vine-water-status-part-2.pdf)
- [Fruit sampling and maturity, British Columbia Wine Grape Council](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/harvest-quality/fruit-sampling-and-maturity/)

### Clones and massal selection

Separates selection, vegetative propagation, grafting and breeding. Health testing is independent of the number of source vines. FPS numbering is an identifier, not a universal performance rank.

- [Sélection clonale, Institut Français de la Vigne et du Vin](https://www.vignevin.com/innovation-varietale/selection-clonale/)
- [Grape selections and selection numbering, Foundation Plant Services at UC Davis](https://fps.ucdavis.edu/fgrrussell.cfm)

### Canopy and yield

Separates crop removal from leaf removal and recognises timing, heat protection and vine capacity. A legal yield ceiling is not a parcel's ideal crop; grape mass and wine volume use different units.

- [The role of canopy management in vine balance, Oregon State University Extension](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/pub/em9071)
- [Canopy management, British Columbia Wine Grape Council](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/vineyard-maintenance/canopy-management/)
- [Grapevine fruit zone leaf removal, Penn State Extension](https://extension.psu.edu/grapevine-fruit-zone-leaf-removal)

### Harvest and sorting

Uses representative sampling and the intended wine style. Hand and mechanical picking have different advantages. Sorting selects what its criteria can detect and does not reverse all damage.

- [Fruit sampling and maturity, British Columbia Wine Grape Council](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/harvest-quality/fruit-sampling-and-maturity/)
- [Standard method for vineyard sampling at harvest, Australian Wine Research Institute and Australian Grape and Wine](https://www.agw.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Method-for-Sampling-Grapes-in-the-Vineyard-for-Assessing-Berry-Composition-at-Harvest.pdf)
- [Harvesting, British Columbia Wine Grape Council](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/harvest-quality/harvesting/)

### Steep slopes and terraces

Separates aspect from gradient and patamares from vinha ao alto. Erosion, access and drainage are taught without treating difficulty as a quality score. Producer material supports description of its landscape, not global engineering limits.

- [Cultura da Vinha, Instituto dos Vinhos do Douro e do Porto](https://ivdp.pt/pt/vinha/cultura-da-vinha/)
- [Vineyard landscaping in the Douro Valley, Taylor Fladgate](https://www.taylor.pt/en/what-is-port-wine/the-douro-valley/vineyard-landscaping)
- [VDP: Mosel vineyard landscape](https://www.vdp.de/en/the-wines/the-vdpregions/mosel)
- [IVDP: Douro vineyard soil protection and drainage](https://www.ivdp.pt/pt/docs/SUVIDUR/Folheto.pdf)

### Old vines and planting density

OIV's 2024 recommendation is identified as a recommendation rather than universal label law. Barossa's separate charter is named. Planting density is calculated from spacing; age, replacement vines and survivorship are kept distinct.

- [OIV VITI 703 of 2024, definitions and recommendations on old grapevines and vineyards](https://www.oiv.int/index.php/node/3806)
- [Old Vine Charter, Barossa](https://www.barossa.com/wine/vineyards/old-vine-charter/)
- [Soils and vine spacing, British Columbia Wine Grape Council](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/vineyard-establishment/soils/)

### Altitude and the diurnal range

Separates absolute and relative elevation, daily range, actual temperature and season. Inversions and fog can reverse a simple higher-means-colder explanation. Radiation and berry exposure are distinct from air temperature.

- [Argentine wine content guide, Wines of Argentina](https://api.winesofargentina.org/uploads/2022/12/YR8TPeyo4V_CONTENT_GUIDE_2023_ING.pdf)
- [Vineyard site selection and thermal belts, North Carolina State Extension](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/north-carolina-winegrape-growers-guide/chapter-4-vineyard-site-selection)
- [Napa Valley regions, Napa Valley Vintners](https://wine.napavintners.com/region)

### Sea, fog and wind

Separates fog, wind and coastal moderation. NIWA's dry inland Marlborough description qualifies broad maritime shorthand. Saline tasting impressions do not establish direct transfer of sea salt.

- [Napa Valley Vintners: regional climates and appellations](https://wine.napavintners.com/region)
- [NIWA: the climate and weather of Marlborough](https://niwa.co.nz/climate-and-weather/regional-climatologies/marlborough)
- [Wine Marlborough: climate](https://www.marlboroughwinenz.com/climate)

### Soils and drainage

Separates texture, structure, rooting depth, drainage, storage and aeration. Nutrient uptake is not literal rock flavour. Soil pH and wine pH are not interchangeable.

- [NC State Extension: vineyard site selection](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/north-carolina-winegrape-growers-guide/chapter-4-vineyard-site-selection)
- [British Columbia Wine Grape Council: soils](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/vineyard-establishment/soils/)
- [The wines of Chablis: minerality](https://www.chablis-wines.com/tasting/minerality,3310,16328.html)

### Ageing terms on the label

Local minimum periods, release calendars and certified average ages are separate clocks. Red Rioja rules are separated from white and rosé. Vessel choice and condition are checked independently of the category.

- [Consejo Regulador DOCa Rioja: classification](https://riojawine.com/en-gb/the-designation/classification/)
- [Consorzio del Vino Brunello di Montalcino: production rules](https://www.consorziobrunellodimontalcino.it/en/586/il-brunello)
- [Consejo Regulador Jerez: special categories and age certification](https://www.sherry.wine/sherry-wine/special-categories)

### Oxidative ageing and rancio

Separates deliberate oxidative development, flor, sweetness and faults. Côtes Catalanes' stated rancio sec minimum is not generalised worldwide. Oloroso is treated as dry, with sweetened related styles checked separately.

- [Consejo Regulador Jerez: Oloroso](https://www.sherry.wine/sherry-wine/dry-sherry-wines/oloroso)
- [Vins du Roussillon: regional styles and rancio sec](https://www.roussillon.wine/vins-et-terroirs/nos-singularites/)

## Verification notes

Sources were researched and opened on 23 October 2026. The published pages and linked documents were checked for the specific concepts used above. Two Penn State pages returned access-denied responses when opened after their indexed primary text was retrieved: the spring frost article and the fruit-zone leaf-removal article. They remain supplementary reading; the frost and canopy lessons each also cite two successfully opened institutional sources. No rule depends solely on an inaccessible page.

Taylor's United States path intermittently timed out. Its successfully opened English path is the one saved in the lesson. The IVDP drainage leaflet and VDP regional account add direct support for terrain management and the Mosel example. The Demeter lesson links both the standards landing page and the actual 2026 standard, including its preparation requirements; philosophy is not adopted as scientific evidence.

Appellation and certification pages are snapshots of the published guidance on the review date. They do not replace the applicable legal specification or a bottle's approved market label. In particular, organic, old vine, Reserva, Riserva and rancio terminology is kept tied to its own jurisdiction or scheme. Agricultural examples explain principles rather than prescribe irrigation, frost-system operation or earthworks.
