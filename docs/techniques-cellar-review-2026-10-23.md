# Cellar technique companion review

Reviewed: 23 October 2026.

## Scope and delivery

This review covers the 14 roster entries at zero-based positions 15 through 28, from `whole-bunch` through `training-pruning`. Authoring is confined to `.scripts/regions/fragments/techniques.2.json`. It does not change existing region records, runtime code, generated output, card identities or publication state.

The fragment contains 112 cards and 112 questions. Each technique has six Certified-level base cards/questions, one Advanced and one Master card/question; six of its questions are multiple choice. Each also contains four referenced deeper paragraphs distributed across at least three reading sections and both deeper levels. Teaching prose is approximately 650 to 740 words per entry, excluding cards, questions, pronunciation and source metadata.

The lessons connect the method to plausible sensory consequences and an appropriate service explanation. Sensory tendencies are separated from guarantees, and a producer-specific example is not treated as a rule for an entire region. No videos, living-person profiles, copied diagrams or copied textbook passages are included.

## Evidence approach

Sources are original institutional, research, extension, regional authority or producer publications. Each record includes dated source links and at least two distinct URLs. The `primary` flag is reserved for standards, government/regulatory and regional/heritage authority material, not applied automatically to every research or producer page.

Pages and PDFs were opened where accessible and compared with their indexed text. Some institutional servers denied or timed out on a direct fetch despite readable indexed content; those exceptions are recorded below. Access on this review date does not imply that an old technical paper is newly published or that an old production specification is current law.

## Record review

### Whole bunch

Distinguishes intact clusters with stems, whole-bunch pressing and strict carbonic maceration. Explains mixed free-juice and intact-berry pathways. Stem flavour and texture are tendencies; no varietal or percentage guarantee. Controlled comparisons are preferable to inferring process from perfume.

Sources:

- [Whole-bunch fermentation: treatment notes, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/winemaking-practices/winemaking-treatment-whole-bunch-fermentation/)
- [Understanding whole-bunch fermentation, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/s2008/)

### Extraction: soaks, punching down and pumping over

Separates pre-fermentation cold soaking, cap operations during fermentation and later maceration. Colour and tannin extraction are not one curve. No prescribed number of pump-overs, temperature or universal pressing date.

Sources:

- [Cold soak: treatment notes, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/winemaking-practices/winemaking-treatment-cold-soak/)
- [Traditional cap management techniques, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/s2350.pdf)
- [Fermentation temperature and red wine composition, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Technical_Review_Issue_252_Godden.pdf)

### Malolactic conversion

Distinguishes the malic-to-lactic conversion from diacetyl associated chiefly with citric acid metabolism. Completion needs analytical evidence. No universal buttery result, acidity target or timing recipe. The AWRI factsheet contains an apparent unit inconsistency in a sensory-threshold passage; numerical thresholds were not reproduced.

Sources:

- [Using malolactic fermentation to modulate wine style, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/files/attachment/mlf_modulation_awri_fact_sheet/)
- [Timing of malolactic fermentation, UC Davis Viticulture and Enology](https://wineserver.ucdavis.edu/industry-info/enology/fermentation-management-guides/malolactic-fermenation/timing-fermentation)
- [Advanced wine assessment course notes: diacetyl, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/01-AWAC-Course-Notes-28092022-1.pdf)

### Oak

Separates extractable wood compounds, vessel geometry, repeated use and maturation conditions. Coconut-like lactones are not a reliable geographical identifier. Does not repeat the UC Davis page’s broad assertion about other wine additions. No universal new-oak percentages or quality ranking.

Sources:

- [Oak lactones, UC Davis Waterhouse Lab](https://waterhouse.ucdavis.edu/whats-in-wine/oak-lactones)
- [Introduction to wine: oak and maturation, Wine Australia](https://www.australianwine.com/getcontentasset/b71a1ced-ae7b-47e5-9b4d-af188ac79d01/2a6464f5-7547-4f96-aacf-a0c3da1718f8/awd_introtowine_consumerguide.pdf?language=en-AU)

### Lees ageing and bâtonnage

Separates retaining sediment, stirring it, autolysis over time and the contributions of other cellar decisions. Yeast-derived compounds contribute within a complex matrix; a creamy sensation alone cannot identify bâtonnage. No fixed stirring schedule or automatic butter aroma.

Sources:

- [Understanding lees contact in white wine, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/249-December-2020-Technical-Review-Godden.pdf)
- [Enology Notes 25: yeast lees, Virginia Tech](https://www.enology.fst.vt.edu/EN/25.html)

### Sur lie

Separates a general method from origin-specific label conditions. The INAO printable page includes internally inconsistent summaries of bottling timing, so no exact calendar window is taught from it. Use the current full specification for a legal-date question.

Sources:

- [Lees ageing in Muscadet, Vins de Nantes](https://www.muscadet.fr/les-vins-de-nantes/lelevage-sur-lie/)
- [Muscadet Sèvre et Maine sur lie, INAO](https://www.inao.gouv.fr/node/37679/printable/print)
- [Enology Notes 25: yeast lees, Virginia Tech](https://www.enology.fst.vt.edu/EN/25.html)

### Governo

Identifies the material introduced into a young wine and its renewed fermentation role. Distinguishes dried grapes or must from ripasso grape residue and from ordinary blending. The 2019 Chianti production text is explicitly historical; the ministry’s 2026 consolidated specification landing page is linked without claiming its PDF was fully inspected. Producer 2015 and 2023 material is not extrapolated to every current wine. A technical-sheet yield conversion was not used.

Sources:

- [Chianti production text, Italian Official Gazette, 2019](https://www.consorziovinochianti.it/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/GU-185-2019-DM-25.7.19-Disciplinare-produzione-vini-Chianti-docg-estratto_compressed.pdf)
- [Governo all’Uso Toscano: current wine account, Cecchi](https://www.cecchi.net/en/wines/governo-all-uso-toscano/)
- [Chianti Governo technical sheet, Cecchi, vintage 2015](https://www.cecchi.net/public/files/39/chianti-governo-uso-toscano-technicalsheet_3.pdf)
- [Chianti consolidated specification publication, MASAF, 2026](https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeBLOB.php/L/IT/IDPagina/24768)

### Chaptalisation and acidification

Distinguishes enrichment, chaptalisation, sweetening and fortification. Separates pH from titratable acidity. OIV recognition does not confer local permission. No universal dose, vintage exemption or combined-use permission is stated.

Sources:

- [Wine production guidance, United Kingdom Food Standards Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/wine-production/wine-production)
- [Chemical acidification of must, OIV](https://www.oiv.int/standards/international-code-of-oenological-practices/part-ii-oenological-treatments-and-practices/musts/chemical-acidification)

- [Titratable acidity and pH in Australian wine, Australian Wine Research Institute](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/s2329-Australian-trends-Titratable-acidity-pH.pdf)

### Amphora and qvevri

Distinguishes vessel material from skin-contact method and honours regional Georgian variation. UNESCO heritage recognition is not a wine-quality certification. Does not repeat claims that burial eliminates temperature variation, that clay metals universally provide antiseptic protection, or that every qvevri uses an identical contact time.

Sources:

- [Qvevri wine, Georgian National Wine Agency](https://wine.gov.ge/En/KvevriWine)
- [Traditional Georgian qvevri method: cultural heritage listing, UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ancient-georgian-traditional-qvevri-wine-making-method-00870)

### Blending (assemblage)

Covers grape, parcel, vessel and year components; a single-variety wine may combine lots. Separates co-fermentation from blending finished wines. Australian claim rules are presented only as a jurisdictional example, without exporting thresholds worldwide.

Sources:

- [Blending Champagne, Comité Champagne](https://www.champagne.fr/en/about-champagne/how-champagne-is-made/blending-champagne)
- [The art of blending, Bordeaux Wine Council](https://www.bordeaux.com/en/know-how/blending/)
- [Blending and mixing, OIV](https://www.oiv.int/node/2596/download/pdf)
- [The blending rules, Wine Australia](https://www.wineaustralia.com/labelling/further-information/the-blending-rules)

### Sous voile

Separates living surface yeast, settled lees and simple oxygen exposure. Compares Jura and Sherry without merging their production systems. A Château-Chalon 2026 draft encountered during research is a proposal, not proof of current law; its proposed grape or ageing changes were not taught as effective. No fixed Vin Jaune ageing or volume-loss formula is supplied.

Sources:

- [Vin Jaune and its yeast veil, Jura Tourisme](https://www.jura-tourism.com/vivre-le-jura/deguster/vins-du-jura/vins-jura-appellations/vin-jaune/)
- [Château-Chalon wine and maturation account, Domaine Berthet-Bondet](https://berthet-bondet.com/vins/blancs-sous-voile/chateau-chalon/)
- [Biological ageing and the veil of flor, Sherry Wines](https://www.sherry.wine/news/biological-ageing-sherry-veil-flor-part-1)

### Fining, filtering and sulphur

Separates fining, physical filtration and sulphur protection. Distinguishes free and total sulphur dioxide and the relevance of pH. Vegan and allergen questions require actual bottling information; there are no medical claims, universal sulphur limits or dosing instructions.

Sources:

- [AWRI: fining agents](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/frequently_asked_questions/fining_agents/)
- [OIV: filtration](https://www.oiv.int/node/2312)
- [Iowa State: sulphur dioxide measurement in red wine](https://www.extension.iastate.edu/wine/sulfur-dioxide-measurement-and-overestimation-red-wine)

### Fermentation and yeast

Separates alcoholic fermentation from malolactic conversion, and inoculated starts from resident microbial populations. Nitrogen availability, strain and environment interact. A 2008 AWRI heatwave investigation is used as a dated example of interacting causes, not a current universal restart protocol.

Sources:

- [AWRI: yeast choice](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/winemaking-practices/winemaking-treatment-yeast-choice/)
- [UC Davis: fermentation management](https://wine.ucdavis.edu/industry-info/enology/fermentation-management-guides/wine-fermentation/fermentation-management-practices)
- [AWRI: yeast assimilable nitrogen](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/wine_fermentation/yan/)
- [AWRI: heatwave and stuck fermentation case study](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/tn-tr175.pdf)

### Training and pruning

Distinguishes current shoots, matured canes, short spurs, older cordons and trellis hardware. Shoot positioning is not one pruning method. Regional advice illustrates vine balance, renewal and winter-injury considerations; no universal bud count, yield ranking or pruning calendar.

Sources:

- [University of Minnesota: dormant pruning and vine structure](https://enology.umn.edu/sites/enology.umn.edu/files/2024-03/pruning_grapevines_hand_out.pdf)
- [Penn State: cane and spur pruning fundamentals](https://extension.psu.edu/grapevine-cane-and-spur-pruning-fundamentals)
- [Comité Champagne: pruning the vine](https://www.champagne.fr/fr/decouvrir-le-champagne/elaboration-du-champagne/la-taille-de-la-vigne)
- [Iowa State: grapevine pruning](https://www.extension.iastate.edu/wine/grapevine-pruning)

## Retrieval limits and excluded material

- The AWRI whole-bunch treatment page repeatedly timed out directly; its indexed institutional text was available, and the separate AWRI whole-bunch research PDF was opened. The broken guessed technical-review attachment was discarded.
- The UNESCO qvevri page returned a challenge on opening; its indexed UNESCO entry confirmed the 2013 heritage listing. The Georgian National Wine Agency page was opened independently. Heritage claims remain narrow.
- The AWRI titratable-acidity and pH paper was readable in indexed PDF text but a direct fetch timed out. The lesson uses the analytical distinction, not a numerical result from a particular survey.
- Penn State’s cane-and-spur page returned HTTP 403 on opening; its indexed text was available. The University of Minnesota pruning PDF and Comité Champagne pruning page were opened and provide independent structural and regional support. Iowa State’s pruning article was opened during the initial research pass; later retries timed out.
- The ministry’s 2026 Chianti consolidated-specification landing page was opened, but the linked PDF was not successfully retrieved. The lesson explicitly avoids presenting the earlier 2019 publication as a complete current rulebook.
- The INAO Muscadet printable overview is unsuitable as sole evidence for an exact bottling window because different passages describe the timing inconsistently. The general lees-contact and designation distinction is retained; exact dates are omitted.
- Proposed Château-Chalon and Côtes du Jura amendments encountered in search are excluded from claims about effective law.

## Assessment review

Multiple-choice distractors were reviewed after drafting. Unrelated packaging, restaurant furniture and other giveaway alternatives were replaced with nearby winemaking or viticulture confusions: sensory evidence versus chemical endpoints, cane versus cordon, cap contact versus lees contact, legal specifications versus incomplete producer descriptions, and separate oak variables. Correct answers and the teaching intent remain stable. Card positions and roster order are unchanged.

Short answers carry explicit accepted variants. Question identifiers are omitted for the established builder to assign. No test fixture or synthetic source was used as authored content.
