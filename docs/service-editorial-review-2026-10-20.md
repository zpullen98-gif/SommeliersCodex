# Service and pairing editorial review

Reviewed 20 October 2026 for the Codex learning review.

## What changed

The pass followed the service references, their flashcard backs and quiz explanations, all 26 structured pairing scenarios, and the Culinary Companion. It also sampled the related Floor, tasting, visual studies and Cellar Compass records to find repeated claims and preserve the existing learning connections. It is not a new certification of every question or a check of current restaurant stock.

- Service guidance now separates an observation from a diagnosis. Air is not a guaranteed remedy for sulphurous aromas; unexpected haze needs assessment rather than reassurance based only on a clean smell or the word unfiltered.
- Removed the glassware claim about directing wine to a separate taste zone. The learning point is useful glass shape and space for aroma.
- Decanting distinguishes sediment removal from a trial with air. Timing, aeration and bottle condition are assessed together.
- The old red, fragile Burgundy and very old bottle rows now use that same condition based approach. Age alone does not dictate that air will improve or spoil a wine.
- Champagne uses Comité Champagne's 8 to 10 C starting recommendation. Cooling times are estimates, cold does not remove cork pressure, and the freezer is not the recommended method.
- Red serving temperature is framed as perception and balance, not a fixed chemical change to tannin. Removed fixed warming rates. Sake guidance recognises producer advice and JSS's varied serving methods.
- Added the fifty year Tawny Port category and distinguished an approved age indication from a vintage or the age of every component.
- Table upkeep now respects drinking pace, refusals and confirmation of another order.
- Edited 24 of the 26 pairing explanations. The two already qualified explanations, jamón and turbot, remain. The model choices and selected priorities are unchanged, but the explanations now discuss actual bottles, food balance, alternatives and guest preference. Removed numerical sugar/alcohol guesses, universal regional failures, age guarantees and claims that bubbles or tannin scrub fat away.
- Added three Culinary Companion lessons: `bottle-check`, `condition`, and `guest-care`. Each includes a short reading, a recall question, a model response, a spoken rehearsal and visible source links.
- Added direct source links to the affected service tables. Existing print references remain named; no invented online copy is implied. The service gate checks safe web URLs separately from visible British prose, so a publisher's URL spelling is preserved.
- Appended three service flashcards; no existing card index moved. All 22 service question IDs, stems, options and keyed answers remain unchanged. All pairing IDs, options, model choices, levels and priority sets remain unchanged.

## Evidence and editorial boundaries

These are primary or institutional sources consulted for the changed claims. The service conversations are original teaching scenarios applying those sources; they are not quotations from Brennan's SOP. Exact house bottles, prices, vintages, measures and approved pairings remain owned by the canonical house pack and lineup.

| Topic | Source and use |
| --- | --- |
| Reduction | [AWRI: removal of volatile sulphur compounds](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/storage-and-packaging/pre-packaging-preparation/removal-volatile-sulfur-compounds/). Different compounds respond differently to oxygen; removed promises of a simple tableside cure. Winery treatments are not instructions for restaurant service. |
| Sulphur dioxide | [AWRI: Advanced Wine Assessment Course notes](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/01-AWAC-Course-Notes-28092022-1.pdf). Distinguishes sulphur dioxide from hydrogen sulphide and other sulphur compounds. No sensory threshold is used to promise allergy suitability. |
| Cloudiness | [AWRI: hazes and deposits](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/fining-stabilities/hazes_and_deposits/). Multiple possible causes require identification. The lesson asks staff to escalate uncertainty. |
| Glassware language | [NIDCD: Taste Disorders](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/taste-disorders). Taste qualities are not confined to separate tongue zones. No claim that glass shape has no sensory effect is made. |
| Decanting | [WSET: serving and decanting questions](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2024/july/21/frequently-asked-questions-about-serving-and-decanting-wine/). Separates removal of deposit from disputed or bottle-dependent aeration benefits. |
| Temperature | [WSET: serving temperatures and storage](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2022/april/26/ideal-serving-temperatures-and-top-tips-for-wine-storage). Useful ranges, excessive chilling and warmth, and ice-water contact. |
| Champagne | [Comité Champagne: serving fact sheet](https://www.champagne.fr/system/files/2022-10/Serving%20Champagne.pdf) and [Union des Maisons de Champagne: chilling](https://maisons-champagne.com/en/houses/the-art-of-tasting/before-opening-the-bottle/article/the-proper-way-to-chill-champagne). Starting range and chilling method. |
| Sake | [Japan Sake and Shochu Makers Association: serving sake](https://japansake.or.jp/sake/en/basic/how-to-serve-sake/). Chilled, ambient and warm styles, including controlled warming methods. |
| Port | [IVDP: Portuguese definitions](https://www.ivdp.pt/pt/comunicacao/guias-tematicos/enologia-e-praticas-enologicas/guia-enologia-e-praticas-enologicas/capitulo-1-definicoes/) and [Portuguese style introduction](https://www.ivdp.pt/pt/vinhos/vinhos-do-porto/introducao/). These include fifty years; several English IVDP pages still show the older four-category list. |
| Order confirmation | [CMS Americas: service standards](https://www.mastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/CMS-Hospitality-Service-Standards.pdf) and [WSET service films](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/videos/wset-wine-service-series). Presentation and confirmation underpin the original bottle-check rehearsal. House-specific details still require the current SOP. |
| Vegan suitability | [WSET: vegan wine](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2024/january/11/what-is-vegan-wine-and-how-to-find-it/). Fining methods vary; verify the exact product. Its broad statement about removal of fining agents is not used as an allergy guarantee. |
| Allergy distinction | [Food Standards Agency: allergies and intolerances](https://www.food.gov.uk/food-safety-and-hygiene/food-allergies-intolerances-and-coeliac-disease). Vegan labelling is not an allergy guarantee. This is a general distinction, not a claim that UK labelling law governs Brennan's. |
| Sulphites | [TTB: wine labelling checklist](https://www.ttb.gov/system/files/images/wine-label/wine-labeling-checklist.pdf). No added sulphites can coexist with naturally occurring sulphites. No numerical threshold is taught as safe for an individual. |
| Pairing | [WSET: pairing principles](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2023/july/13/four-rules-to-masterful-food-and-wine-pairing/), [winter pairing](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2020/december/21/winter-wine-and-food-matching), and [drinks with spice](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2026/how-to-pair-drinks-with-spice). Used as sensory starting points, then applied to original dish scenarios. They do not establish a single objectively correct bottle. |
| Barbera wood ageing | [Consorzio Barbera d'Asti: technical sheet](https://www.viniastimonferrato.it/denominazioni/barbera-asti-docg/). Its technical section allows different barrel sizes. Wood ageing is not itself evidence of strongly apparent new oak. The page's prose and technical ageing-duration text disagree; no new total-duration claim was added. |
