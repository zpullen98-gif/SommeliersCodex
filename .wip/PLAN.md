## The durability rule (new, binding on every agent)
- Work happens on pushed work-in-progress branches: Codex `codex54-wip` (from master 680a33c), WorldTable `card-sale-wip` (from dish-standards 05991cc). Each agent commits by explicit path with the trailer and pushes its branch after every finished section, and before it ends its turn. A reclaim then costs at most one agent's unpushed minutes.
- Orchestration lives in the branches too: this plan as `.wip/PLAN.md` and the workflow scripts as `.wip/*.workflow.js` on `codex54-wip`; the staged chunks under `tools/house/brennans/_staging/` on `card-sale-wip`. The publish step removes `.wip/` and `_staging/` in its final commit, then merges each branch into master or dish-standards (fast-forward, or a merge commit if the base moved; never a rebase or force push).
- No agent ends its turn while a command runs in the background; every long command runs in the foreground with a long timeout; its final message is its report.
